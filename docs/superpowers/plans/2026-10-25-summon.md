# Summon Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `infinite_craft:summon`: right-clicking an item calls helpers that fight for the player only, never hurt him, another player, a pet, a villager or each other, and vanish after their time.

**Architecture:** A clamped record component (`SummonComponent`) like the other abilities. `SummonAbility` spawns the mobs and `Summons` owns the rules: a per-player registry (cap 12), the countdown, and the guards. Friendly AI reuses vanilla goals: each summon's `targetSelector` is cleared and refilled with owner-guarding target goals, and the griefing goals are stripped from its `goalSelector`. Two choke points enforce safety whatever goal or projectile acts: `MobEntity.setTarget` (no forbidden target) and `ServerLivingEntityEvents.ALLOW_DAMAGE` (no forbidden damage).

**Tech Stack:** Minecraft 1.21.10, Fabric, yarn, Mixin; Fabric game tests (server) and client game tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-variety-and-playtest-design.md`, "Part C" → "`infinite_craft:summon`" and Judges 7, 10, 11. This is the first of three Part C PRs (summon, grapple, build).

## Global Constraints

- Builds use `-Werror` (no deprecated Fabric API). JDK: `export JAVA_HOME=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk@21/libexec/openjdk.jdk/Contents/Home`, run `sh ./gradlew`.
- New ability code follows `src/main/java/com/infinite_craft/ability/`: a record component with a codec whose constructor clamps every field, plus a tooltip line, registered in `Abilities`. Item NBT parsing is lenient, so defaults must give a working item.
- `README.md` and `AiPrompt.java` are CRLF; edit them byte-safe (Python, `newline=''`). Prompt text blocks contain literal `\t` escapes. New files are LF.
- Fields and clamps (spec): `entity` default `minecraft:wolf`, must be on the allow-list, else the default; `count` 1, clamp 1 to 6; `seconds` 60, clamp 10 to 180; `name` optional; `gear` optional mainhand and head item ids, items only, no NBT; `size` 1, clamp 0.5 to 2; `color` optional, same codec as `shoot`.
- At most 12 summons per player at once; summoning past that removes the oldest.
- Summons vanish with a puff when their time runs out, when he logs off, or when he changes dimension, and spawn on free ground, never inside blocks.
- Never spend API credits: the user runs the eval (`claude-opus-5-5`).
- BehaviorSignature: add tokens only for the new field (`summon(<mob>)`); `baselineVarietyIsPinned` must keep passing.
- Workflow: branch `summon` off `main`, PR on the fork, wait for "merge it".

## Rulings made while planning

- **Allow-list** (spec leaves the detail to the builder): wolf, iron_golem, snow_golem, bee, fox, polar_bear, llama, zombie, husk, drowned, skeleton, stray, bogged, vindicator, pillager, vex. Dropped from the spec's list:
  - goat, axolotl and piglin run on Brain AI, which ignores target goals, so they couldn't be made to fight for him;
  - blaze fireballs set blocks alight;
  - cats have no attack.

  Cost if wrong: a wish for a goat helper falls back to a wolf.
- **Bees keep their stinger.** A vanilla bee dies after one sting, so summoned bees get `hasStung` reset after each sting.
- **Summons are `setPersistent()`**, so they never despawn naturally and they stay in Peaceful (a `checkDespawn` mixin). They are still saved with the chunk, so a summon found on chunk load that isn't in the registry is discarded (it was left over from a restart).
- **"Color"** is a colored dust puff when a summon arrives and leaves, plus a faint dust trail every 10 ticks. There is no team glow, which would need a scoreboard team.
- **Built-in cooldown:** 3 s minimum. A longer `minecraft:use_cooldown` from Claude wins.

## Review Focus

1. **Conversions:** a summoned zombie in water (to drowned), a husk (to zombie) or a skeleton in powder snow (to stray) must not turn into a wild mob that attacks him. A test asserts that `convertTo` on a summon returns null and that the summon remains.
2. **Crossfire:** a summoned skeleton or pillager shooting past the player hits the husk, never him or his wolf (`skeletonShotsNeverHurtOwnerOrPet`).
3. **His own side:** when he swings at a summon by accident, his tamed wolf (`AttackWithOwnerGoal`) and a nearby iron golem must leave it alone (`ironGolemAndTamedWolfLeaveSummonAlone`).
4. **Spam:** right-clicking 10 times in a row gives at most 12 summons, and the cooldown holds (`spamClickingStaysAtTwelve`).
5. **Tight spaces:** summoning an iron golem in a 1-wide, 2-high tunnel never puts it inside blocks (`golemInATunnelNeverSuffocates`).

### Task 3: On his side: friendly AI and the two guards

**Files:**

- Test: `src/gametest/java/com/infinite_craft/test/SummonTest.java`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 2's `Summons.isSummon`, `ownerOf`, `Holder`.
- Produces:
  - `Summons.mayTarget(MobEntity attacker, LivingEntity target) -> boolean`.

    For a summon attacker, it's false for: any player, any `Tameable` with an owner, any `MerchantEntity` (villagers and traders), any other summon, and any mob that isn't a `Monster` unless it attacked him or the summon. For an attacker that is an `IronGolemEntity`, a `SnowGolemEntity` or a tamed `Tameable`, it's false when the target is a summon.
  - `Summons.mayDamage(LivingEntity victim, DamageSource source) -> boolean`, where the attacker is `source.getAttacker()`, which covers arrows and other projectiles.

    It's false when the attacker is a summon and the victim is a player, a tamed pet, a merchant or a summon. It's also false when the victim is a summon and the attacker is its owner or the owner's tamed pet.
  - `SummonGoals.apply(MobEntity mob, PlayerEntity owner)`:
    - clear `targetSelector` and add, in order:
      1. whatever attacks the owner;
      2. whatever the owner attacks;
      3. revenge on whatever hurt the summon;
      4. the nearest `Monster` within 16 blocks of the owner;
    - remove `StepAndDestroyBlockGoal`, `MoveThroughVillageGoal`, `BreakDoorGoal` and `DoorInteractGoal` subclasses from `goalSelector` (`GoalSelector.clear(Predicate)`; reach the protected fields through an `@Accessor` mixin on `MobEntity`);
    - add a follow goal: walk to him past 10 blocks, teleport beside him past 24.
